How to Choose Perfect Custom Gift for Loved Ones

How to choose a custom gift for someone you love: think about daily use, pick a personal detail and check spelling. Examples with prices.

A custom gift works best when it fits the person. A few simple questions make the choice easier.

Start with daily use

Think about what they carry or use every day. A pen and keychain set or a name wallet gets noticed again and again.

Pick the personal detail

Choose a name, an initial, a date or a short message. Short text looks cleaner on small items such as rings and keychains.

Match the budget

Our customized items start at Rs. 600 and deal boxes go up to Rs. 2,500, so you can pick something that suits the occasion.

Check spelling before you order

Spelling mistakes are the most common problem with personalized gifts. Read the name aloud, send it in the chat and we confirm it before production.
