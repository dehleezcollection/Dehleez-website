Frequently asked questions

How do I customize a product?

Open a product, fill in the options (name, text, size or design) and tap Order on WhatsApp. Your details arrive in a chat with us.

How long does it take?

Most products are ready in 5 days. The full Gift Deal takes 7 working days and production starts from the next working day. Some items are still to be confirmed and are marked on the product.

Can I place an urgent order?

Frames have an urgent option at Rs. 200 extra. For other products, ask us on WhatsApp.

What happens after I send my order?

We confirm the price, timeline and order details. Production begins after confirmation, then we do a quality check before delivery.

Can I request a different color or size?

Yes, message us. Rings need your size, and plating or color choices are listed on the product.

Are packing and delivery charges separate?

We share product price, any customization charges and delivery or packing charges in the quotation before you confirm.

Can I order more than one item?

Yes. Send all items in one message and we will confirm everything together.

How do I check my spelling?

Double-check names and text before sending. We will also confirm the spelling with you before production starts.
